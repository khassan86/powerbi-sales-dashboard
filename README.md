The goal was to create a Power BI Dashboard for a Fintech company called "LikeCard" to facilitate analyzing and viewing the company's sales performance. We have divided the dashboard into 3 tabs, each of them is specialized in visualizing a different business category. The 1st tab shows the company performance as a whole, the 2nd tab shows the product performance, and the 3rd tab shows the employees' performance in terms of target and achievement.
Each tab serves as an identifier to one of the company's critical metrics. In the 1st tab, we wanted to know the Profit margin, in  the 2nd one, we wanted to know the cost of products and the product mix. In the 3rd one, we wanted to know the target achievement.
The dashboard has multiple filters and slicers to focus on cross-functional categories and multi-layer analysis.
The dashboard can be connected to a real-time database to show numbers instantly, or it can be fed with a dataset manually to reflect pre-prepared numbers.
